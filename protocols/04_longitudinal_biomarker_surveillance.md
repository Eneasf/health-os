# Protocol 04: Longitudinal Biomarker Surveillance Guidelines

## 1. Venous Testing Schedule
- **Baseline Panel:** Comprehensive 86-analyte metabolic, endocrine, lipid, and hematological baseline prior to new training phases.
- **Mid-Protocol Surveillance (Week 8):** Safety review (Hematology, Hepatic ALT/AST, Renal eGFR/Creatinine, High-Sensitivity CRP).
- **Consolidation Panel (Week 16):** Full evaluation of physiological adaptations against baseline.

---

## 2. Phlebotomy Conditions & Quality Control
- **Fasting Requirement:** 10–12 hour overnight fast with adequate ad libitum hydration (water only).
- **Timing:** Morning draw between 08:00 and 09:30 AM to standardize circadian rhythms.
- **Exercise Precaution:** Avoid vigorous resistance training for 48 hours prior to phlebotomy to prevent transient exercise-induced elevations in AST, ALT, Creatine Kinase, or hs-CRP.
- **Methodology:** Strict venous draw by an accredited phlebotomist; finger-prick capillary tests are rejected for clinical tracking.
