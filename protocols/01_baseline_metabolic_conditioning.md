# Protocol 01: Baseline Metabolic Conditioning & Tendon Resilience

## 1. Overview & Objectives
- **Phase Duration:** 16–24 Weeks
- **Primary Objective:** Build aerobic mitochondrial density, joint tendon resilience, and baseline metabolic efficiency.
- **Biomarker Surveillance:** Quarterly venous blood panel (Metabolic, Lipids, Renal, Hepatic, CBC).

---

## 2. Evidence-Based Nutritional Supplementation
All compounds administered at clinical baseline doses:
- **Creatine Monohydrate:** 5g daily (morning with water or post-workout meal). Promotes intramuscular phosphocreatine resynthesis.
- **Omega-3 EPA/DHA:** 2000mg daily (with meal containing dietary fat). Autonomic stability, cellular membrane fluidity, and triglyceride control.
- **Vitamin D3 + K2:** 5000 IU daily (morning). Calcium homeostasis, bone mineral density, and immune modulation.
- **Magnesium Bisglycinate:** 400mg daily (30–60 minutes prior to sleep). Promotes neuromuscular relaxation and slow-wave sleep architecture.
- **Hydrolyzed Whey Isolate:** 40g post-workout. Muscle protein synthesis and leucine threshold saturation.

---

## 3. Training Periodization & Autonomic Recovery
- **eGym Resistance:** Standard Mode (2x/week), progressive overload.
- **Cardiovascular Base:** 150 minutes/week Zone 2 aerobic cardio (conversational pace, HR 115–130 bpm).
- **Autonomic Redline:** If 7-day rolling HRV drops >15% below baseline or RHR elevates >5 bpm for 3 consecutive days, volume is halved for an active deload week.
