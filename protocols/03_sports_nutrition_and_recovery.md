# Protocol 03: Sports Nutrition & Recovery Architecture

## 1. Daily Macronutrient Breakdown (Hypertrophic Phase)
- **Caloric Strategy:** +250 to +400 kcal clean surplus above measured TDEE.
- **Protein Ceiling:** 2.2 to 2.6 g/kg total body weight partitioned across 4 equal meals spaced 3.5–4.5 hours apart.
- **Dietary Fat:** 0.8 to 1.0 g/kg (20–25% of total caloric intake) prioritizing monounsaturated and omega-3 sources.
- **Complex Carbohydrates:** Remainder of daily caloric target to maximize intramuscular glycogen storage.

---

## 2. Fluid Balance & Autonomic Monitoring
- **Hydration Baseline:** 35–45 mL/kg daily water intake adjusted for sweat rate.
- **Fluid Retention Triage Checklist:**
  1. Peripheral edema or sock-ring ankle indentation
  2. Resting systolic blood pressure spike (+10–15 mmHg)
  3. Rapid unexplained overnight mass shift (>1.0 kg)
  4. Disrupted sleep architecture or acute facial puffiness
- **Triage Action:** If >= 2 criteria are confirmed, pause sodium load and verify renal function markers before continuing caloric surplus.
